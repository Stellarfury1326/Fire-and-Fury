📜 HISTORICAL ARCHIVE

🌋 FOUR ERUPTION STYLES

HAWAIIAN
Generally associated with highly fluid lava and relatively gentle, steady flows.
STROMBOLIAN
Known for intermittent bursts caused by gas bubbles breaking through magma.
VULCANIAN
Short explosive eruptions can produce ash-rich clouds when viscous magma or a plug is disrupted.
PLINIAN
Powerful sustained eruption columns can rise many kilometres into the atmosphere.

🚀 MYSOREAN ROCKETS

18TH CENTURY
Mysorean forces developed and used iron-cased war rockets associated with the armies of Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan.
ANGLO-MYSORE WARS
British forces encountered Mysorean rockets during conflicts with Mysore.
CONGREVE ROCKETS
British officers studied Mysorean rocket technology. William Congreve later developed a British military rocket system.
HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Mysorean rockets form an important chapter in military rocketry. Modern space rockets have a much broader and later technological history.
