📜 HISTORICAL RECORD

🌋 Four Eruption Styles

Hawaiian
Generally characterized by relatively gentle, highly fluid lava flows and low explosivity.
Strombolian
Known for intermittent bursts caused by gas bubbles breaking through magma.
Vulcanian
Short, explosive eruptions can produce ash-rich clouds and fragments when viscous magma or a plug is disrupted.
Plinian
Very powerful sustained eruption columns can rise many kilometres into the atmosphere and spread ash widely.

🚀 Mysorean Rockets

18th Century
Mysorean forces developed and used iron-cased war rockets, associated with the armies of Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan.
Anglo-Mysore Wars
British forces encountered these rockets during conflicts with Mysore.
Congreve Rockets
British officers studied Mysorean rocket technology. William Congreve later developed a British military rocket system.
Historical Significance
These developments form an important chapter in the history of military rocketry. Modern space rockets have a much broader and later technological history.
